Common questions

How often should a generator be serviced?

Most standby generators need a service at least once or twice a year, plus regular test runs. The right interval depends on the engine and its running hours, and we set it in your AMC plan.

What is a load bank test?

A load bank applies a controlled electrical load to the generator so we can confirm it delivers its rated output, and that cooling, voltage and frequency stay stable.

What does an ATS do?

An automatic transfer switch detects a mains failure, starts the generator and moves your load across, then returns to mains when power is restored.
